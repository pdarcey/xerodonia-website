About Upcoming Birthdays

Upcoming Birthdays is a simple app to let you see the upcoming birthdays of the just people you care about

Built with a focus on performance, privacy, and a clean user experience.

Features

  • Beautiful, intuitive interface
  • Widgets to show upcoming birthdays, and birthdays in the coming week
  • Works on iPhone, iPad and Mac
  • No data collection or ads
  • Offline-first experience

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Upcoming Birthdays

Upcoming Birthdays does not collect or transmit any personal information. No data leaves your device.

We do not access or transmit any data in any way.

There are no advertising, or tracking frameworks used in this app.

We do use an analytics framework in this app to gather anonymous information about app crashes, etc. but we do not collect any information at all about you or your contacts.
