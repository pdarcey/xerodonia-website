Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Distance Mapper

Distance Mapper does not collect or transmit any personal information. All data is stored locally on your device.

If you enable iCloud sync, your data will be stored securely on your iCloud account using Apple's infrastructure. We do not access or transmit this data in any way.

There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks used in this app.

You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or using the in-app reset option.
